# Age ratings

Fill both store questionnaires with the same answers. The app has no user-generated public content, no ads, no purchases, and no chat.

Expected rating: **4+**.

Jyotish / rashifal is cultural-educational, not “horror” or “mature themes.” Do not tick those.

## Google Play (Content rating questionnaire)

Use the **IARC** questionnaire in Play Console:

- Category: **Lifestyle** (or Reference if asked)
- User interaction: users can sign in; **no** public UGC, **no** social features that share with strangers
- No violence, sexual content, drugs, or gambling
- Location: used for panchanga, not shared publicly
- Target age: **Everyone**

Expected: **Everyone** / PEGI 3.
