# **COMPREHENSIVE YAJURVEDA CATALOG: ADHYAYAS 1–40**
### *A Complete Reference Guide to Rishis (Seers), Devatas (Deities/Subjects), and Chhandas (Meters)*

---

## **OVERVIEW OF THE YAJURVEDA SAMHITA**

The **Yajurveda Samhita** (specifically the Shukla Yajurveda / Vajasaneyi Samhita) consists of **40 Adhyayas (Chapters)** containing **1,975 Mantras**. Unlike the Rigveda (which is arranged into 10 Mandalas and 1,028 Suktas), the Yajurveda is structured chronologically by ritual, philosophical, and cosmic themes across 40 Chapters.

Each Mantra or section within an Adhyaya is traditionally assigned:
1. **Rishi (ऋषि)**: The seer or sage who revealed/realized the mantra.
2. **Devata (देवता)**: The subject matter, divine power, or central theme of the mantra.
3. **Chhanda (छन्द)**: The poetic Vedic meter governing the verse structure (e.g., Gayatri, Trishtup, Jagati, Anushtup, Brihati, Pankti).
