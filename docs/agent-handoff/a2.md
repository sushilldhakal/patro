## Left in the apps, and why

These pairs were not moved. Geometry and copy for several of them already live in `packages/features` and `packages/domain`; the drawings are still per app.

- **Wheels and charts** (`PanchangaWheel`, `WheelChart`, `AvakahadaWheel`, `GocharKundaliChart`, `D1Chart`). Radii, labels and body maths are already shared (`panchanga-drawing`, `wheel-chart-geometry`, `wheel-bodies`). The drawings still depend on app assets (Vite `?raw` / `?url` glyphs, `apps/mobile/assets`, bundled fonts, pan/zoom). Two SVG halves belong in the kit behind one props contract; that move is not done. `wheelPalette` is the shared colour half.
- **Layout and sections** (`PanchangaLayout`, `PanchangaSections`, `PanchangaSidebarNav`, `PanchangaMonthGrid`, `GhatiClock`, `DayTimeline`, `PlanetSelectMenu`, `NavataraBalamCardGrid`). Large and wired to app routers, location hooks and glyph icons. `PanchangaSection` is imported by `apps/web/src/components/kundali/KundaliReport.tsx` and `KundaliView.tsx` (Agent 3) and by `apps/mobile/app/(tabs)/panchanga/_layout.tsx` (layouts are off limits). Any move must leave a re-export at the old path.
- **Graha chrome** (`GrahaPlanetIcon`, `GrahaStatusBadges`, `GrahaStatusLegend`, `GrahaStatusMarksSvg`, `GrahaPageParts`). Planet artwork is app SVG assets with hand-typed fills; the kit forbids hex, so the glyphs cannot be copied in as-is. Status badges and page parts are imported from kundali, rashifal, home and `DayDetailModal` (other agents). A re-export shim is required.
- **Gochar sections** (`GocharIngressSection`, `GocharSkySection`, `GocharRashyadiTables`, `GocharPlanetDeepDive`) and **dainik kranti tables** (`MonthLagnaMatrix`, `MonthGrahaSpashta`, `MonthCalcNotes`, and the day-patro tables). Same pattern as the aside panels (features hook + class map) but not moved in this pass.
- **`ElementGlyphIcon`, `CalendarMoonPhaseIcon`**. Outside importers (home, rashifal, kundali, learn, `BsCalendarGrid`, `TodayHighlightCard`). Keep a one-line re-export if they move. `MoonPhaseIcon` has no importers outside its own files; it can move with the wheel.
- **`PanchangaVivaranPanel`, `MuhurtaAsidePanel`, `GrahaDetailsList`**. They live under `components/home` and `components/kundali`, which this agent does not own. Not touched.
- **`D1Chart` on the website** is `apps/web/src/components/kundali/D1Chart.tsx` (Agent 3). The app copy is `apps/mobile/components/panchanga/D1Chart.tsx`. `GocharKundaliChart` on the website imports the kundali chart.
