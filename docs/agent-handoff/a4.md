## Still two copies, and why

- **`EclipticWheel` and `GuideGrid`**, including `apps/mobile/components/learn/playground/EclipticWheel.tsx`. Each file builds a Three.js object graph (`Group`, `LineSegments`, `Mesh`, materials, `depthTest`, `renderOrder`) that a WebGL scene parents. An SVG stand-in would not be a mesh the day scene or Aakash Gochar can mount. Radii, `atLonInto`, `BELT_MID`, `NAK_MID`, and `ECLIPTIC_GRID_COLOR` are shared. The playground file still inlines its own belt builders.
- **`DaySimScene`**, **`TwoSystemsScene`**, **`AakashGocharScene`**, **`AakashGocharSky`**. Three.js scenes, textures, and the render loop. The sky's compass and time sheet are shared; the scene is not.
- **`hips-config`** and **`sun-earth-moon-math.ts`**. Texture and pixel-versus-unit maths for those scenes. Not a 2D control.
- **`DayChapterBar` / `DayChapterWelcome` names** remain on the two binder files. The drawing is the kit. The names stay because the playgrounds import them from the app path.
- **`TwoSystemsStudy` names** remain on the two files that own the canvas, gestures, and fullscreen shell. The control panel and its copy are `TwoSystemsPanel`.
- **`ChapterOverlay` binders** still look up each app's `LEARN_DIAGRAMS`.
